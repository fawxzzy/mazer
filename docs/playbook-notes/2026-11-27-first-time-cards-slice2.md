The host polls the lit Switches each frame and fires on a gate going from unlit to lit (a level first seen starts from none lit, because lit state is per attempt and in memory, so the step that installs the host counts; nothing fires while not playing; a Switch relit after a reset never re-fires once the card was seen). The ring and its "Open edge" label go on the opened border cell nearer to the player by walking distance. The Guide `bleed-gate` heading "Wrap gate" becomes "Switch" (D1) and its text is the card body: "A Switch is the pale tile with a dashed ring. Walk onto it to open a hidden wrap." The drift test, player-message inventory, the options-guide label list and a sheet-plan comment follow.
  - Card spot: the card takes an edge that covers none of the player, the ring and the goal (a cell under the header never counts as covered by a top card). When both edges cover one (seen on real L22 boards: player under the header, goal just below it, ring at the bottom), it slides to the free band nearest an edge. Only when no band is free does it take the edge covering the least (weights player 3, ring 2, goal 1).
  - Tests: new `tests/tutorial/first-time-host.test.ts`; `tests/tutorial/first-time-cards.test.ts` live-list and wrap coach pins.
- WHY it changed:
  - First-time cards spec (docs/design/MAZER-FIRST-TIME-CARDS-SPEC-2026-11-26.md) slice 2, with Zac's picks of 2026-11-27 (card look E Two-line, skin B Neon edge, keep the dotted ring on the thing pointed at, never cover anything important).
- Rule: scene glue that needs private MenuScene members reads them structurally and pins the member names with a source-scan test, so a rename fails a test instead of silently drawing nothing.
- Pattern: the host owns the auto-dismiss clock (frames while visible) instead of the DOM timer, so a pause or the Guide never eats the card's on-screen time.
- Decision: caps reset when the auth scope changes (a new session per scope). Shard notices keep their own notice host, so in the hazard lab a shard hint can stack with a card.
- Decision: a ring on a cell under the header keeps the ring but drops its label: the coach mark puts the pill below such a ring, which landed "Open edge" on the menu button on a real L200 board. The card names the mechanic anyway.
- Failure Mode: a placement rule tested only on synthetic numbers covered the goal on real boards; drive real boards (both engines) and read the notice rect against the player and goal before claiming "covers nothing".
- Decision: the wrap ring sits on the edge cell the player walked off (the cell they arrived on is under the player).
