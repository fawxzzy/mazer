- WHAT changed: Two changes, one lane. (1) MAZER-CTRL-005 stick/keyboard speed parity plus the leash check: the floating-stick held-step interval is no longer capped at 122 ms, so full stick deflection steps at the speed profile's repeat interval (151 ms at the default speed, 132/113/92 ms at 0.3/0.6/1), the same interval a held keyboard uses. The keyboard now chains its steps off the ideal step time (new pure helpers `resolveKeyboardStepFloorBaseMs` and `resolveKeyboardStepAnchorMs` in `src/legacy-runtime/legacyHeldStepCadence.ts`) instead of the OS key-repeat clock, so a 60 Hz frame or a 33 or 83 ms OS repeat no longer stretches it. The leash (`FLOATING_ANCHOR_LEASH_INCHES`, baked as `DEFAULT_LEASH_BAKE_PX` in `src/render/controllerHaloView.ts`) goes from 90 px to 56 px (56/160 in). New harness `tests/reset/legacy-stick-keyboard-speed-parity.test.ts`. (2) MAZER-VIS-057 landscape corner stars: `src/render/edgeCornerAnchor.ts` gains `isRoundedLandscapePhoneViewport` (landscape, height < 500, any reported safe area); such a phone gets the same 20 px tip inset on all four corner stars, and its bottom pair is treated as rounded even when only a side inset is reported. `src/render/edgeMarkers.ts` `EdgeViewport` and the scene's `resolveLegacyEdgeStations` pass the left and right safe areas. Hot files: `src/scenes/MenuScene.ts` (narrow: imports, repeat-cap constant removed, keyboard step anchor, edge-station safe areas), `src/render/edgeMarkers.ts`, `src/render/edgeCornerAnchor.ts`.
- WHY it changed: Measured in the deterministic harness, a full stick deflection stepped every 122 ms (8.18 cells/s) while a held keyboard at the same speed setting really stepped about every 167 ms (about 6.0 cells/s): two different speeds for the same setting. The 90 px leash sat beyond the 33 px phone stick radius, so a finger had to travel almost three radii before the anchor followed it; 56 px is the stick's own reach plus a small margin (28 px on the halo bake, 0.35 in). On a landscape iPhone the status bar is hidden, so all four canvas corners are rounded display corners; the top pair used the square-corner rule and was cut off while the bottom pair cleared the rounded corner, so the two pairs disagreed.
- Rule: There is one movement speed per setting: stick and keyboard both step at `movementSpeedProfile.repeatIntervalMs`, and the only tuning knob is `LEGACY_MOVEMENT_STEP_RATE_FACTOR`; never add a stick-only or keyboard-only cap, and never add a variable-speed drag toggle (not in scope, CTRL-007 lock kept). Placement of a corner star comes from the measured safe area in `edgeCornerAnchor.ts` only; a landscape window with no safe area, desktop and iPads (height >= 500) keep their square top corners. New cosmetic draws use `withCosmeticColours`; Reduce Motion keeps the colour.
- Evidence: Parity measured in cells/s, stick = keyboard at every speed: 6.63 (151 ms, default), 7.57 (132 ms), 8.86 (113 ms), 10.86 (92 ms); it holds at 60 and 120 Hz, at OS key repeats of 33 and 83 ms, and at every deflection from 14 to 140 px. Before: stick 8.18, keyboard about 6.0. Default speed therefore moves keyboard +10 percent and stick -19 percent; `LEGACY_MOVEMENT_STEP_RATE_FACTOR` retunes both at once. Landscape stars: new tests in `tests/render/edgeCornerRoundedDisplay.test.ts` cover 844x390, 852x393, 932x430, a notch-side-only 812x375 and home-indicator-only cases (no painted or lit pixel in a hidden R48 to R66 corner, whole orbit lap, real pixels at DPR 3), iPads, and the unchanged desktop and square-corner cases. Headless chromium and webkit captures at DPR 3 (844x390 with simulated 47/21 safe areas, 932x430, iPad 1180x820, desktop, Reduce Motion): all four corner stars sit the same distance from their side edge (about 25 css px), the bottom pair is lifted for the home indicator, no overlap with the menu button, colour kept under Reduce Motion. Nothing checked on a real phone: stick feel at the new speed and the 56 px leash, and the rounded-corner fit on a real iPhone and iPad, need a phone.
