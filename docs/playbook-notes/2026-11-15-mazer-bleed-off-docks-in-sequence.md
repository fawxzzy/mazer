# 2026-11-15 Bleed-off docks are drawn out in sequence with the maze (follow-up to MAZER-MOTION-020)

- WHAT changed: the bleed-off paths (the baked Chip docks outside the board rect that carry a corridor off the edge) no longer pop in and out on their own clock. `src/render/mazeRevealTimeline.ts` replaces the global `resolveDockRevealExtent` / `MazeRevealFrame.dockExtent` (one number for all docks, only after the build ended) with `resolveDockReachExtent(direction, window, ownerRank, tileCount, lengthCells, elapsedMs)`: a dock is one more stretch of line after the border cell that owns it. Build: it starts when its owner cell is whole (`cellStart + rampMs`) and grows outward at the speed of the line (one cell per rank step, capped at `MAZE_DOCK_FADE_MS` for the whole dock). Tear-down is the same schedule run backwards: the dock retracts into its cell just before that cell starts to go. `src/render/boardRevealMask.ts` no longer multiplies the owner cell by a dock extent, so a border cell that owns a dock follows its own rank like any other cell. `src/render/boardTileLayer.ts` (`composeBoardTileRevealSoft`) draws each dock on its own: every margin strip is cleared, then each dock is redrawn from the baked canvas, anchored at the board edge and shown to its own depth; the strip is split between the docks of a side at the line half way between them, device-snapped with shared boundaries, so no device pixel is drawn twice and there is no seam. `src/render/mazeRevealDriver.ts` hands the layer a per-dock `dockReach(x, y, lengthCells)`; `src/scenes/MenuScene.ts` only drops the `boardTileDockOwners` argument of that call (that array is still used by the Reduce Motion / cells path). The legacy 0/1 edge-path switch (`bleedProgress`, glow and trail) is unchanged. Reduce Motion is unchanged: no soft reveal, the 200 ms board crossfade. Nothing is faded; a dock is only ever a longer or shorter run of solid tile.
- WHY it changed: owner, 2026-11-15: "the part of the maze gen or deconstruct for the bleed off paths they kinda just appear id like for them to smoothly get drawn out in sequence with everything else as well". After MOTION-020 the corridors flowed, but every dock-owning border cell was held back until the whole build ended and the four margin strips then slid out together (and at tear-down all docks vanished first), so the paths that run off the edge appeared and disappeared as one block, out of step with their neighbours.
- Rule: a bleed-off dock is not a separate object with its own clock; it is the continuation of its owner cell and takes its timing from that cell's rank (start = owner whole, speed = one cell per rank step, tear-down = reverse). Any new piece of the board that lives outside the cell grid must be scheduled from a cell rank in `mazeRevealTimeline.ts`, never from a second timer. The margin must be split between docks of one side so two docks never draw the same device pixel. New `tests/render/bleedOffDockReveal.test.ts` pins the ordering and determinism of the schedule (build and tear-down, every size, docks one by one in rank order, linear and monotonic, whole inside the build frame, outside-order cells never show a dock, bad input safe) and the layer drawing (independent docks, disjoint strips, a not-yet-started dock is not drawn, depth anchored at the board edge on every side).
- Evidence: headless Chromium, 390x844, DPR 3, touch, standalone, virtual clock (`scripts/analysis/capture-maze-motion-filmstrip.mjs`, new `--docks N` / `--crop` options for a per-dock zoom sheet). Build, level 40, a top dock of rank 249 sampled every 100 ms: the corridor grows up out of the board edge from t=1800 to 2300 ms in step with its neighbours and the dock cap lands at 2300 ms with no pop. Tear-down, level 40, a top dock of rank 293 sampled every 70 ms: dock and cell are present at 1020 ms and gone by 1090 ms with the corridor retracting downward cell by cell at 1090 to 1230 ms. Mazes are random per load, so the sampled dock differs between runs.
- Status: headless only; nothing checked on a real phone. Reduce Motion is covered by the unit tests (no soft reveal frame) and was not re-captured.
