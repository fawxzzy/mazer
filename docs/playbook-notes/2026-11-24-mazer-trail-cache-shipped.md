- WHAT changed: MAZER-PERF-017, Zac picked C + A on the trail-cost options page (#704), and it is now the game's default Star Dust painter. `src/render/starDustTrailCache.ts` is what `MenuScene.drawTrailCanvasSegments` calls (`drawStarDustTrailToCanvasContext`, same name as before). On a long trail (48 tiles or more) everything older than the newest 8 tiles is painted once into an offscreen layer with today's full layers (bloom, thread, keyline, fills). That layer is the trail canvas's exact size, so the bloom grid, stretch and clip match a full paint. Each frame copies the layer in and paints only the live head with A's lighter bloom (`LIGHT_BLOOM_PLAN`, moved to `src/render/starDustTrailPlan.ts`). Short, fading, over-budget and no-DOM trails are painted whole, exactly as before, issuing the same canvas calls (unit test). The layer is rebuilt when the route changes before the cached split (new level, reset, undo/backtrack, trimmed start), when the head grows past 8 more tiles, every 2 s, when the animation clock goes backwards (restarted scene), and on any change of device scale (DPR, zoom, backing budget), tile size (resize), keyline colour (theme) or lifecycle alpha, Trail Shine / reduced motion, Trail Fade's origin advance, or a canvas origin move that is not whole device pixels. Star Dust's own colours are fixed per time (cosmetics keep their colours under dark mode), so the 2 s clock covers them. It is released on scene shutdown and never grows past the trail canvas's capped backing size (1.6 Mpx, the WebKit canvas memory cap). Pause/resume keeps the layer while the route and style are unchanged. The options page stays dormant (not a build input; row "C + A (shipped)" clears the seam so the game's own painter runs). Probes: `scripts/analysis/check-star-dust-trail-cache-parity.mjs` (pixel diff vs a full paint) and `scripts/analysis/trail-perf-options-bench.mjs` (now with the CA row).
- WHY it changed: on WebKit the bloom and keylines of a long trail were about half of a walking frame at level 300 (2026-11-24-mazer-trail-cost-options.md). Caching the old trail removes most of that per frame, and A's bloom makes the remaining head cheaper. Accepted trade-offs (Zac's pick; the stars stay real, pointed and rainbow-shifting, never glow): older sparkles hold still between rebuilds; their colours and the shine band move in steps (at most every 2 s); the old trail's age taper can be up to 8 tiles stale.
- Rule: a cached layer that will be blitted under a live paint must be painted at the target canvas's exact size and origin. Cropping it to the route bbox changed the blur grid and put 24% of pixels off at 3x. Prove a render cache with a pixel diff against the full paint outside the live region, at fractional DPRs, in both engines. Playwright WebKit builds are tied to the Playwright version: when `newPage` hangs, check `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ms-playwright` for the build the installed `playwright-core` expects (repo 1.59.1 wants 2272, this machine only had 2395), and pass a matching Playwright with `--pw-module=<file url>` instead of reinstalling browsers.

- Follow-up (review fixes, 2026-11-25): (1) Breath phase. The trail's breath (thread width x1.0-1.1, glow alpha x0.7-1.0, 3 s cycle) was baked into the cached layer at whatever phase the rebuild landed on, so every 2 s rebuild jumped the old trail: max channel step 56 (Chrome) / 72 (WebKit) outside the head, about 30% of lit pixels off by more than 16, at 393x851 x2.625 (3x the same). Fix: the cached layer is now painted at the steady breath (0.5, what reduced motion already holds) and only the live head breathes. After: rebuild step outside the head 0 in both engines at x2.625 and x3; at the head seam max 3, the same as one frame of the full painter. Trade-off: the older trail no longer breathes (it sits at mid-breath, up to max 21-49 / mean ~5 from a full paint at the breath peak); the head still does. Probe: `scripts/analysis/check-star-dust-trail-breath-seam.mjs`. (2) The cache hot path no longer allocates per frame: no head slice (start index), no key string (field compare), one reused overlay object, the device scale is passed in (no `getTransform`), the route is checked by first/split vertex plus one rotating 128-segment window per frame, and cached sparkles under the head come from a candidate list built at rebuild. Whole-paint calls are unchanged (tests). (3) The coarse-bloom scratch canvas is released on scene shutdown and when the trail skin leaves Star Dust. (4) Canvas growth by whole device pixels (the backing grows down or up, the layer is reused at the offset): no clipped bloom line at the old edge (edge band max 0-1 in both engines). The reused layer can differ from a grown-canvas paint by up to 75-99 over the bloom, but the full painter itself changes by the same amount when its canvas grows (its bloom downscale grid follows the canvas size; measured 30-149), so the cache adds no new step, it only moves it to the next rebuild. Unit test: `tests/render/starDustTrailCacheFollowups.test.ts`.
- Rule: every animated input to a cached layer is either in the cache key or held at a fixed value in the layer; a value that changes every frame must never be baked at the phase the rebuild happens to land on.
