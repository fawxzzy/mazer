# 2026-11-15 MAZER-PROG-020 S3 board-aware fill floor, and the GEN-021 goal-pick floor fix

- WHAT changed: (1) `resolveGeometryBudgetBoardFillFloor(fillFloor, boardCells)` (`legacyGeometryBudget.ts`) scales the density-fill floor of a level by the board's cell count over the 25x51 reference board (1275 cells), never down; the lifecycle passes it the real base maze size (25x57 at level 1, toward 27x59). (2) `resolveFarthestReachableFloor` (`legacyMaze.ts`, now exported) takes the route floor as a 4th argument: when the farthest reachable tile reaches `minimumSolutionPathLength`, the span-aware goal pick can only choose a tile at or past that floor. Before, the pick's only floor was the current sub-minimum route length, so a tile still under the 1.5x floor could win on span. Regression test: `tests/reset/legacy-goal-span-floor.test.ts`.
- WHY it changed: PROG-020 grows the board, and the fill floors were absolute cell counts measured on the old board, so the bigger board read emptier (MAZER-GEN-021 real-maze audit). The goal-pick floor was a reviewer finding on GEN-021.
- Rule: the fill only carves branches whose re-run explorer step count is exactly unchanged, so scaling its floor moves the look and never the felt ladder (windows, ordering and the ladder `--assert` hold). Generation changes regenerate `tests/fixtures/progression-ladder-golden.v1.json` from the code, never by hand.
- Measured (12 seeds per cell, both boards, play profile, S2 head vs S3): fill share +1 to +3 points (L60 phone 30.5 to 32.4, L140 phone 32.4 to 35.0, L300 phone 39.4 to 41.1); 5x5 blank windows roughly halved at L60 and L200 and near zero at L300; 0 one-tile stubs everywhere; route over linear size unchanged (2.6 to 3.3).
- Goal-pick fix on the sampled corpus: no change in any output (golden identical before the fill change, census identical), because the bug needs a re-placed goal under the floor whose span beats every tile at the floor; it is guarded by the unit test on a constructed board, not by a corpus case. Per-maze route floor, 600 seeds x levels 10-12 x 2 boards: min 1.38 (desktop level 12 seed 10368948) and 1.46 (desktop level 10), 2 of 3600 mazes under the 1.5x generation floor; they lose route to the wrap carve and tuner after the goal is fixed. The reviewer's 0.76-1.05 minimum was not reproduced with the census seeds or with 600 other seeds (min 1.38).
- NOT done (S3 follow-ups, proposed): felt-target scaling by area ratio (touches the calibration GENERATED blocks and the ceiling; audit risk 'difficulty cliff'), `straightBranches: false` and turning fill branches (the comb texture), a goal-distance floor above 1.5x, loop-shape rules. Phone look not done.
- Generation time (CPU user ms per 8-level pass, `measureLevelGeneration`, minimum of 4 passes, 3 alternating rounds, machine under other load so absolute numbers drift): origin/main 6172 / 6500 / 6625, S2 head 8000 / 6172 / 6781, S3 6453 / 6219 / 6391. Best-quiet pair: main 6000 vs S3 6219 (+3.7 percent); within the 10 percent budget but the noise floor is of the same order, so treat it as 'no measurable regression', not a precise figure.
