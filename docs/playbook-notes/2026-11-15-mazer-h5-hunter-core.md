# 2026-11-15 Mazer slice H5 core: Hunter engine, joint verdict, replay (MAZER-HAZ-005, pure logic)

- WHAT changed: three new pure modules in `src/mazer-core/hazards/`, exported from the hazards index:
  - `hunterEngine.ts` (`mazer-hunter-engine-v1`): `resolveHunterSpeedDivisor(tier)` (tier 0 = divisor 3, tier 1 = 2, never below 2), `createHunterSpec(den, { tier, graceTurns, spawnDelayTurns })` (chaser rule), den rule `listHunterDenCandidates(graph, { start, goal })` (static distance from the start >= ceil(0.6 x route), out of the start's sight lines, >= 2 steps from the goal; split into off-route and on-route cells) and `selectHunterDen(graph, { start, goal, seed, index, attempt, existingDens, excludeCells })` (prefers off-route cells, keeps 6 steps from other dens, seed from `resolveHunterDenSeed(hazardSeed)`), runtime `HunterState` with `createHunterState`, `stepHunter`, `hunterCell` (-1 once burned out) and `registerHunterContact` (third contact burns the Hunter out, A4; contact with a burned-out Hunter throws).
  - `hunterRun.ts` (`mazer-hunter-run-v1`): the play-time rules. `createHunterRunContext({ graph, specs, hunters, start, goal })`, `createHunterRunState`, `stepHunterRun(context, state, action, { probe })` returns `{ state, events }`. Wall bump or closed gate = WAIT (A1); a gate closing on the player ejects them one trail cell back, no contact, no invulnerability (A3); a Hunter or Sentry contact rewinds to the last junction strictly behind the contact cell on the player's own trail, falling back to an earlier junction or the start when the joint search finds no route from it, with 4 invulnerable ticks (A2). Events: `contact`, `burnout`, `eject`, `goal`. Replay: `encodeHunterInputLog` / `decodeHunterInputLog` (letters U L D R W, `mazer-hunter-input-log-v1`), `replayHunterRun(context, log)` and `playHunterRun(context, policy, maxTicks)` return a `HunterRunResult` with per-tick frames and an FNV-1a `digest` of events + frames.
  - `hunterVerdict.ts` (`mazer-hunter-verdict-v1`, `mazer-hunter-overlay-v1`): `checkHunterJointVerdict(graph, specs, hunters, { start, goal, stateCap })` returns `pass | fail | unknown` with reasons (unknown first). It runs the P6 chaser validator at N = 2 with state cap 200k (witness + post-contact) and `measureHunterEscapeSlack` (escape slack >= 0.9, no fragile step in the first 10). `computeHunterOverlay(graph, { start, goal, hazardSeed, allocation, baseSpecs })` adds Hunters one at a time on top of the H2 gates and Sentries, up to 3 den attempts each, and degrades with a reason (`no-den`, `verdict-failed`, `verdict-unknown`, `enemy-cap`).
  - Analysis: `scripts/analysis/hunter-bot-distributions.ts` (`--levels 35,60,150,300 --seeds 4 --assert --out <json>`).
- WHY it changed: roadmap slice H5 (Hunter) needs its rules proven deterministic and solvable before anything is drawn or wired.
- Rule: the joint escape slack is A3-aware. `analyzeChaserFairness` treats a WAIT on a gate that closes next tick as lost; under A3 it is an eject, so the H5 verdict uses `measureHunterEscapeSlack` and calls the chaser validator with `includeFairness: false`. Without this, gate levels that H2 accepts were rejected even with no Hunter.
- Rule: the validator checks post-contact routes at 2 invulnerable ticks; play grants 4 (A2). The rewind target in play is the last junction strictly behind the contact cell.
- Rule (for wiring): H4 also defines a rewind. The two must be unified when H5 is wired (one trail, one junction test, one invulnerability counter).
- Evidence: tests `tests/core/hazards/hunter-run.test.ts` (tick-by-tick agreement with the independent reference `hunterRunReference.ts` on 3 fixtures x 25 seeds x 160 random actions; burnout at 3; replay identity and digest), `hunter-engine.test.ts` (den rule, BFS and sight lines against an oracle), `hunter-verdict.test.ts` (pass witness = oracle shortest route and replays clean; unknown on a tiny cap; head-on corridor fails; overlay determinism and caps; A3 slack vs chaser fairness). Bot script, phone board, 2 seeds x 2 boards per level, node on a loaded desktop (6 parallel workers, paging): Hunters shipped 8/8, 8/8, 16/16, 16/16 at L35/60/150/300 (divisor 3, 3, 2, 2), no degrade. Joint verdict median 20.5 / 46.5 / 127.2 / 67.7 ms per level (budget 40: met at L35 only). Planner bots (5% and 15% slips): 0 of 48 runs stranded, median clear ticks 1.01-1.26 x witness, contacts median 0, no Hunter above 3 contacts, every replay digest equal. The reckless probe bot (2-tick lookahead, no plan) stranded 13 of 32 runs and is reported for information only.
- Status: built, not wired (no scene, worker or render). `--assert` fails on the 40 ms verdict median (L60+). Cost is mostly the per-state post-contact loop in `validateLevelWithChasers`; a cached k = 0 layer like H2's `createSolvableQuery` is the likely fix.
