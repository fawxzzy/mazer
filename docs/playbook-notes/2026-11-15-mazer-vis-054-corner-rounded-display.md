# 2026-11-15 MAZER-VIS-054 corner diamonds fully visible on rounded displays

- WHAT changed: `src/render/edgeCornerAnchor.ts` adds `EDGE_CORNER_ROUNDED_TIP_INSET_PX` (16), `isRoundedDisplayViewport` (375 <= width < 500 and height >= 780) and a viewport argument on `resolveEdgeCornerTipInset`; `src/render/edgeMarkers.ts` threads the viewport through `resolveEdgeCornerInset`, `resolveEdgeStations` and `resolveOrbitingEdgeStation`, so each corner diamond slides inward along the diagonal and its outer tip sits 16 css px from both edges on those phones (it still scales about the tip). `src/legacy-runtime/legacyHeaderControl.ts` adds `resolveLegacyHeaderCornerDiamondReach` and `resolveLegacyHeaderLeadingEdgeInset`, and both header sides now clear the moved top diamonds with the UI-035 4 px gap; `src/input-human/touch.ts` passes the viewport height so the hit frame follows. Desktop, 375x667, 360 and narrower, landscape and callers that pass no viewport are unchanged (tip inset 0).
- WHY it changed: owner, 2026-11-15: the corner diamonds look "half off screen" on phones. VIS-044 put the tip exactly ON the screen corner; a display corner of radius R hides 0.293 R per axis (14 px at R48, 16 px at R55), so the diamonds were mostly cut off. 16 px lands the tip on the R55 arc (exact 16.1 px).
- Rule: a corner anchor that is a point ON the screen corner is only right for square displays; on a rounded display the anchor must be where the arc passes the diagonal. Header clearance is derived from the same inset (`resolveEdgeCornerTipInset`), never a second constant, so moving the diamonds cannot silently reintroduce an overlap. New `tests/render/edgeCornerRoundedDisplay.test.ts` models R=55 (and R=48) and fails if any painted pixel of a corner diamond at 50/75/100% lies in the hidden quarter disc.
- Trade-off (measured, not hidden): the header glyphs now sit 39 px in on tall phones (were 16 to 23 px), which narrows the room for the centred title. At 390 wide the header icon scale goes 0.93 to 0.78 and the title footprint goes from 164 to 123 px wide; 393 (0.95 to 0.78) and 412 (0.89 to 0.78) shrink the same way; 430 barely changes (0.89 to 0.87); 375x812 (already 0.79), 375x667, 360 and 320 are unchanged. Capping the diamond (as for EDGE_CORNER_NARROW) only rescued 412, because even a 50% diamond overlaps the old glyph box, so the clearance was kept and the title shrink is reported for the owner to accept or to relax the clearance.
- Residual risk: phones with R above 55 (hides more than 16 px) and continuous-curve (squircle) corners are not exactly a circle; the width/height band is a heuristic for "rounded phone".
- Evidence: `docs/ui/evidence/2026-11-15-corner-rounded-display/` (before from origin/main and after, Chromium DPR3 touch standalone emulation at 390x844, 375x812 and 430x932, 50% and 100%, R=55 cut-away painted magenta).
