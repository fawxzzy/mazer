# 2026-11-15 MAZER-MOTION-020 maze build / tear-down becomes a geometric flow, not a fade

- WHAT changed: the soft maze reveal no longer ramps tile opacity. Each floor cell has an extent 0..1 and the baked board is drawn once at alpha 1 through one clip of whole cells plus one sliced rect per partial cell (`composeBoardTileRevealSoft`, `src/render/boardTileLayer.ts`). The slice starts at the side the cell's BFS parent is on, so corridors extrude out of each other. Tear-down is the build run backwards with the same rank. Walls open only when the least built floor beside them is past 60 %. Docks slide out of the board edge. Bake art unchanged, `BOARD_TILE_BAKE_VERSION` stays v3. Reduce Motion unchanged (200 ms board crossfade).
- WHY it changed: owner request, "I hate the current fade in / fade out ... we can do better"; also folds in the VIS-050 leftover, the thin white 1px line before the fill. Root cause of that line: the baked chip is a pale rim plus a dark fill, so any low-alpha cell shows the rim first. Fix by construction: nothing is drawn at partial alpha.
- Rule: a reveal must not use opacity as its mechanism for a baked two-layer tile (rim + fill); use geometry so every visible pixel is at full strength.
- Rule: the per-cell wipe must equal one rank step (`span / N`, linear). Because a cell's BFS parent has a lower rank, the drawn line is then gapless at any instant on any maze. A longer or eased wipe leaves gaps and a ladder at the tip.
- Rule: a baked wall cell holds the outer glow of every corridor beside it, so open it by the LEAST built neighbour, not the most.
- Concepts compared in headless filmstrips: alpha baseline, iris, slit, scale, flow. Flow won: no ghost frames, no lone rim, one clip + one drawImage per recompose.
- Evidence: Chromium 390x844 DPR3 and WebKit iPhone 15 Pro filmstrips (build and tear-down, level 40) and native-pixel zoom crops from `scripts/analysis/capture-maze-motion-filmstrip.mjs`; tsc, full npm test, playbook:verify, wave-mix and menu-render-frame tests. Headless only, not checked on a real phone.
- Not changed: windows and pacing (`introTimeline.ts`), input unlock, beams, edge-path switch, totals, MenuScene. The gap between tear-down end and the next build (digit interstitial) is lifecycle timing, outside this render-only change.
