# Edge diamonds: outer tip on the screen edge at every size, corners clear of rounded displays (MAZER-VIS-043)

Status: implemented 2026-11-14 on branch `claude/vis-043-20261114`, on top of MAZER-VIS-038 (`docs/ui/MAZER-EDGE-INNER-TIP-SIZE-CYCLE-2026-11-13.md`, PR #512, live). Source, unit, pixel and headless-Chromium evidence only; NOT checked on a real phone.

Owner request (verbatim): "the diamonds on the bottom corners are clipping off screen ensure all diamonds are tip touching the edge of the screen in any position, also when they shrink move them to always be touching edge of screen". This confirms the earlier hypothesis (MAZER-VIS-033 section 3) that the bottom corner diamonds are cut by the rounded display corners, and it SUPERSEDES the VIS-038 rule "scale about the inner tip".

## What changed

1. **Anchor on the OUTER tip.** A diamond scales about its outer tip (`anchorStationToOuterTip`): the outer tip stays exactly where the full-size diamond has it, on the screen edge, at 50%, 75%, 100% and every size in between. The inner tip, where both beams attach, moves toward the board as the diamond grows and back out as it shrinks: `resolveEdgeInnerTip(station, scale)` = outer tip + 20 px x scale along the axis (full size: vertex 0 of the kite; 50%: the station centre). `resolveEdgeBeams` computes the beam endpoint every frame from the diamond's CURRENT size (`resolveEdgeDiamondScale(direction, clock, index)`), so a departure beam aims at a moving target and its head lands exactly on the full-size inner tip at the contact instant (the diamond is exactly 100% then): no jump, pinned by a test that compares the head at `contactMs` and 16 ms earlier. An arrival beam starts on the tip while the diamond drains (still 100%) and follows the tip outward while it shrinks. Fill and drain are unchanged (one fill, inner tip to outer tip, drain the other way) and are drawn inside the scaled outline. Timing, input unlock and the beam flight are unchanged.
2. **Painted tip, not vertex.** The edge-flush maths used the outline vertex plus half a stroke. A canvas strokes with the default miter join, so the sharp outer tip paints `0.5 / sin(atan(7/12))` = 0.99 px beyond its vertex (0.70 px toward the edge for a 45 degree corner kite), i.e. about 0.5 px past the canvas edge. The painted outline is now computed exactly (`resolveEdgeMarkerPaintedPolygon`: half a stroke outside every edge, miters at the vertices) and the stations are placed so that its bounding box touches the edge exactly: `resolveEdgeStationNormalExtent` is 10.99 px for a midpoint (was 10.5) and 7.77 px for a corner.
3. **The painted canvas edge, not the css edge.** The backing store is `round(css x resolution)` device px and the context is scaled by the resolution, which is the DPR rounded to hundredths and capped at 3 (`resolveMazerCanvasResolution`: 2.625 becomes 2.63). At 390x844 that is a 1026x2220 canvas = 390.114 x 844.106 world px, not 390 x 844. `resolveEdgeStations(viewport, resolution)` takes the resolution and places the tips on `round(css x resolution) / resolution` (`resolveEdgeVisibleExtent`); at DPR 1 it is the css size. The scene passes `resolveMazerCanvasResolution()` (one argument added to `resolveLegacyEdgeStations`).
4. **Corners.** Decision made by looking at the rounded-corner overlay (`docs/ui/evidence/2026-11-14-edge-corner-outer-tip/`): a corner diamond whose tip sits on the corner point (or tangent to both edges, 7 to 14 px from the corner) is cut by ANY rounded display corner: a corner of radius R hides everything within about 0.29 R of the corner along each edge, 14 px at R = 48 and far more than the 7.8 px half-width of the kite. The diamonds keep pointing along the corner diagonal and keep their outer tip on the top / bottom edge, but they now sit `EDGE_MARKER_CORNER_CLEARANCE_PX` = 56 css px along that edge from the screen corner (painted extent, `resolveEdgeCornerInset()` = 56 + 7.77 = 63.77 px for the centre): the visible bottom edge of a rounded display starts exactly R px from the corner, so a diamond whose nearest painted point is 56 px away is untouched by any corner up to R = 56 (iPhone 12 to 15 Pro class: about 47 to 55). The layout stays mirror-symmetric (the top corners move too; they were never cut, the status bar is above them in a home-screen app). The ring orbit uses the same inset (the perimeter rectangle is inset by it), so the orbit is continuous and exactly lands on the rest layout after whole steps.
   Documented limit: a corner rounder than 56 css px (some newer phones, 60 to 62) still trims the nearest sliver of a bottom corner tip (a test pins this). `env(safe-area-inset-*)` was considered and NOT used: the safe-area insets describe the home indicator and the notch, not the corner radius, and the layout is deliberately UI-independent (the header doc of `edgeMarkers.ts`). A per-device radius would need a measured value; the constant is one number to tune.
5. **Reduce Motion:** fixed 50% with the outer tip on the edge (the same scale function, no animation); unchanged otherwise.

## Tests (every viewport, every DPR, both board sizes)

- `tests/render/edgeDiamondEdgeContact.test.ts` (new, 58 tests): for 7 viewports (phones 390x844, 393x852, 412x915, 360x740, 375x667 with the 25x51 board; desktops 1280x720, 1440x900 with the 45x27 board; the layout is resolved through `resolveLegacyMenuLayout` to prove the viewport is what the board gets) x DPR 1, 2, 2.625, 3 x every station x 50%, 75%, 100%: the painted bounding box lies inside the canvas (css and device px), touches the edge the station sits on within 0.5 device px, and, on real pixels, a software rasteriser with miter joins draws the real `drawEdgeMarker` with the real context scale: not one lit pixel beyond the canvas border, and the last two device rows/columns of the edge are lit. Also: corner diamonds clear a rounded corner of the clearance radius at every size; and the 62 px counter-example (the documented limit).
- `tests/render/edgeMarkers.test.ts`: painted polygon, anchoring about the outer tip (outer tip fixed, inner tip moves monotonically, 50% inner tip = centre), the beam endpoint follows the current size, head lands on the full-size tip at contact, the scene wiring, orbit never leaves the viewport and stays flush at every phase.
- `tests/render/edgeBeamPixels.test.ts`: per DPR 1, 2, 2.625, 3: the beam endpoint on the moving inner tip at 50%, 75%, 100% (the kite's tip pixel and the beam head pixel are both lit), the head lands on the full-size tip in the real draw path, and the fill stays inside the smaller outline and anchors on its own inner tip at 50% and 75%.
- `tests/render/support/rasterContext.ts`: opt-in `miterJoins` (default off, every other raster test is unchanged).
- `scripts/analysis/verify-gameplay-teleport-transfer.mjs`, real built game, manual clock: the painted outer tip is on the edge at every frame of the grow, hold, launch, drain and shrink (and fixed in Reduce Motion); the beam endpoint follows the scale-aware inner tip; the ring "parked" drift check now tracks the outer tip (the inner tip moves with the size); and a real-canvas probe (diamond layer shown vs hidden on the same frame time) shows each diamond's pixel footprint reaches the last two device rows/columns of its edge and the four corner diamonds are at least 56 css px from the screen corner.
